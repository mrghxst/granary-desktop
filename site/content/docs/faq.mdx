## Is this an official Silo app?

No. Granary is an independent client that is compatible with Silo media servers. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Silo Media L.L.C.

## Does it run on macOS or Linux?

No. Granary relies on Windows HDR, DXGI and Direct3D 11 for the display handling that is its reason to exist.

## Why not just use the web app?

Browsers limit codecs and HDR, cannot switch the display's HDR mode or refresh rate, and cannot pass audio through to
a receiver. Granary does all of that through mpv and puts the display back afterwards.

## Does it phone home?

No. Granary talks only to the servers you add. There is no telemetry and no auto-updater.

## Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later. It bundles libmpv (GPL build)
and FFmpeg; versions and source links are in the third-party notices and on the About screen.

## Was it written by AI?

Largely, yes: with Claude Code, under a written specification and a strict local check suite. See
[How it was built](/docs/how-it-was-built).
