---
title: Audio
description: Output device, layouts, exclusive mode and receiver passthrough.
---

- **Output device**: the Windows default or a specific device.
- **Speaker layout**: stereo (default, downmix), automatic (the device's layout), 5.1 or 7.1.
- **Normalise downmix** (on): keeps surround mixed down to stereo or headphones from clipping.
- **Exclusive audio mode** (off): takes over the device during playback (WASAPI exclusive), bypassing the Windows
  mixer.
- **Audio passthrough** (off): sends Dolby and DTS streams unchanged to an AV receiver. Tick the formats your receiver
  decodes: AC-3, E-AC-3, TrueHD, DTS, DTS-HD.

<Callout>Audio settings apply immediately, also during playback.</Callout>
