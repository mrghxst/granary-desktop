## Starting

Press **Play**. A full-screen launch card with the title's artwork stays up until the first frame is on screen, then
fades away.

The server decides how the file is delivered: **direct play** (the original file), **remux** or **transcode**. Direct
play is the normal case for remuxes and gives the best quality.

## Controls

Move the mouse or press a key to show the controls. They hide on their own, together with the cursor, so nothing
static stays on an OLED panel.

- **Seek bar** with chapter ticks and intro/credits ranges. Hover it for a **preview image**: the server's trickplay
  sheets when it has them, otherwise frames decoded from the file itself (direct play only; turn off under
  **Settings → Player → Seek-bar previews**). For MKV files Granary reads the keyframe index and fetches one keyframe
  per preview, so previews are fine-grained and quick even for large remote files.
- **Audio** and **Subtitles** menus show the language first, then codec, channels and flags such as Atmos, forced,
  SDH and default. Your picks carry over to the next episodes of the same series.
- **Skip intro** and **Skip credits** when the server knows the ranges. Credits followed by an epilogue offer both
  Skip credits and Next episode, and the countdown waits for the real end.
- **Next episode** with a countdown (also across seasons), previous episode. With autoplay off or the countdown
  cancelled, a Next episode button stays at the end.
- Speed, aspect ratio, subtitle delay, picture settings and chapters.
- **Statistics** (`i`): codec, resolution, HDR format, decoder, dropped frames, display mode.
- **Mini player** (`T`) and fullscreen (`f` or double-click).

Media keys and the Windows media overlay work as you'd expect.

## Progress

Granary reports your position to the server while playing and when you stop, so resume points and "continue
watching" stay in sync with your other devices.

## Advanced: your own mpv options

Granary ships tuned defaults. Power users can add `%APPDATA%\Granary\mpv.conf`; it is layered on top of the defaults.
Options that would break the integration (the window, scripts, config loading) are always forced back.
