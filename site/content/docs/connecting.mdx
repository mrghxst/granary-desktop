---
title: Connecting to a server
description: Add a server, sign in and choose a profile.
---

1. On the start screen choose **Add server** and enter its address, for example `media.example.com` or
   `192.168.1.20:8080`.
2. Granary checks the address first. It warns clearly about certificate problems and about plain `http://`.
3. Sign in with your username and password.
4. Pick a profile. Profiles with a PIN ask for it.

You can save several servers and switch between them from **Servers** in the sidebar.

## What happens to your password

- Granary exchanges it for a session once and never stores it.
- The session tokens are kept in **Windows Credential Manager**, scoped to that server's origin.
- The Rust side of the app makes every request. The interface (WebView2) and the player (mpv) never see a token:
  artwork goes through an internal image scheme and video through a loopback relay on `127.0.0.1`.

## Profiles

**Switch profile** in the sidebar returns to the profile picker. Watched state, favourites and ratings belong to the
profile.
