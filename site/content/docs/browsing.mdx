## Home

The server's own home rows: a featured banner, continue watching, recently added and the rest of what your server
offers.

## Libraries

Every library has three views:

- **Recommended**: the server's rows for that library.
- **Library**: the full grid. It keeps loading as you scroll. Sort by title, date added, release date or rating, or
  **Shuffle**. **Filters** narrows by genre and decade, and the **A–Z rail** on the right jumps to a letter.
- **Collections**: the library's collections; open one to see its titles.

Turn on **Settings → General → Quality tags on posters** to see 4K, HDR and audio badges on every poster.

## Detail pages

- Logo or title, year, rating, runtime and the best file's quality, TMDB score, genres, tagline and overview.
- **Directed by** and **Written by** link to the people.
- The **Play** button fills up with your progress and shows the time left; next to it, play from the start.
- **Version**, **Audio** and **Subtitles** pickers choose what plays before you press Play. Tracks always lead with
  their language.
- **Mark watched**, **favourite**, a 1–5 star rating, and the **⋯** menu for watchlist and "not interested".
- **Cast & crew**, grouped into direction, writers and cast; **Trailers & more** (local extras play in Granary,
  YouTube trailers open in your browser); **More like this**.
- Series show seasons and episodes.

## People

Click anyone in the cast and crew for their photo, life facts, biography and filmography on your server, split into
all, movies and series.

## Search

**Search** in the sidebar searches the whole server as you type.
